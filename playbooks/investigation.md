# Investigation

Use this when a question, symptom, or historical decision is not yet explained.

## Inputs

- A focused question and the decision it will inform.
- Available evidence sources and their access limits.
- A time or effort budget.

## Procedure

1. State the question, the decision it affects, and what a sufficient answer would let someone do.
2. Separate facts, inferences, assumptions, and unknowns.
3. Form competing explanations and seek the observation with the highest power to distinguish them.
4. Keep a compact evidence table: claim, source, confidence, and contradiction.
5. Stop when the evidence supports a decision, the budget ends, or a missing source needs a new authority grant.

## Result contract

Record: answer or unresolved question, evidence, confidence, alternatives eliminated, limits, and the recommended next decision.
