# Performance improvement

Use this when a measured cost must decrease without breaking a named behavior.

## Inputs

- A baseline measurement, method, sample definition, and target.
- A behavior contract that must remain true.
- A budget and relevant operating conditions.

## Procedure

1. Reproduce the baseline under documented conditions. Keep the raw sample summary and method.
2. Identify the dominant cost and competing causal hypotheses.
3. Change one justified mechanism at a time; keep a rejected-change record.
4. Re-run the same measurement method and compare it with the baseline.
5. Verify the preserved behavior and look for shifted cost rather than only the chosen metric.

## Stop conditions

Stop when no improvement clears the target with credible evidence, conditions cannot be reproduced, or an optimization changes the behavior contract.

## Result contract

Record: baseline, method, change, new measurement, confidence, behavior proof, and rejected hypotheses.
