# Prototype

Use this when a cheap experiment can settle a design or behavioral fork.

## Inputs

- A decision with at least two plausible options.
- A bounded experiment that exposes the material difference.
- A selection rule known before building.

## Procedure

1. State the decision, options, and the evidence that would select each option.
2. Build only enough of each option to observe the deciding property.
3. Keep experiments isolated from production commitments and label their intentional shortcuts.
4. Apply the declared selection rule and record the result, including a choice to reject every option.
5. Discard or quarantine the experiment unless it becomes an intentional, separately reviewed implementation.

## Result contract

Record: decision, options, selection rule, observations, choice, confidence, and which prototype artifacts were discarded.
